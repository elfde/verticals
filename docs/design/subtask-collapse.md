# Hide the steps without losing the task

A task's inline list ends with Hide, under its last step. Folded, the list leaves Show and its number of subtasks below
the title, where the list was. Both are set at the steps' own size, in the narrow column and the wide one. A click or
keyboard activation folds or unfolds the list without opening the task or changing its hierarchy, dates or completion;
a key press moves the focus to the control it brings.

An open task always shows its steps and offers neither control: it was opened to see them, and a Show over its date
with a Hide under "Add…" made the control jump between the two. Closing it folds the list back as it was.

Hide stands under the list, not over it: under the title it came between a task and its steps, and with one step it took
as much room as the folding saved. The count leaves Hide, since the steps it counts are in view above it.

Opening a step keeps its path visible. Other vertical columns keep their own tasks. The choice is saved on this device
across reloads; a new task starts expanded, and unavailable local storage falls back to the current session.
