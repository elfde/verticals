# Hide the steps without losing the task

A task's inline list ends with Hide, under its last step ("Add…" in an open task). Folded, the list leaves Show and its
number of subtasks below the title, where the list was. A click or keyboard activation folds or unfolds the list without
opening the task or changing its hierarchy, dates or completion; a key press moves the focus to the control it brings.

Hide stands under the list, not over it: under the title it came between a task and its steps, and with one step it took
as much room as the folding saved. The count leaves Hide, since the steps it counts are in view above it.

The task's notes remain available while its list is hidden. Opening a step keeps its path visible. Adding a step reveals
the list. Other vertical columns keep their own tasks. The choice is saved on this device across reloads; a new task
starts expanded, and unavailable local storage falls back to the current session.
